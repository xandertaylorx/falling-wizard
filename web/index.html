01A living world

Nothing here is painted on.

Sand slides into dunes. Water finds its level. Oil floats, burns, and if you've pooled enough of it, explodes. Lava boils lakes into steam and cools into basalt.

Light is ray-traced through the world, so lava lights up caverns and sunlight pours into cave mouths with real shadows.

02An endless Earth

Walk forever. Dig forever.

The world is generated as you explore: oceans and beaches, plains, forests and jungles, deserts and tundra. Below lie caves, ruins and lava caverns. Above, cloud platforms and sky isles.

  • Volcanoes that erupt, geysers that blast
  • Rain, fog, snowfall, sandstorms and lightning that hunts trees
  • A day and night cycle, with fireflies after dark
03Creatures

They die how you kill them.

Lions pounce, demon eyes stare you down, skeletons lob bones, dragons breathe fire and the Devourer tunnels through rock.

Burn one and it crumbles to ash. Strike it with lightning and its skeleton flashes. Freeze it and it shatters. Tame your favourites and they'll fight at your side.

04The spellbook

Sixteen ways to ruin a landscape.

Every spell talks to the simulation. A fireball sets a forest alight. A tornado rips up the topsoil. Frost turns a lava lake to stone.

05Beyond the sky

Then leave the planet.

Fly high enough and the sky gives way to stars. Find a portal structure, tune your wand, and warp at lightspeed to any of an endless galaxy of worlds, each with its own ground, seas, gravity, sky and creatures.

I  TUNE

Right-click with the Portal Wand to choose from the thirty nearest planets. Earth is always on the list.

II  OPEN

Shoot an ancient portal structure: stone arches, crystal rings, basalt monoliths, brick temples.

III  WARP

Step through and cross the galaxy at lightspeed. A gate home is waiting when you land.

Get the game

Your first spell is one command away.

$curl -fsSL https://fw.crooli.com/install.sh | bash

Paste into Terminal on a Mac. It builds the game on your machine (a couple of minutes the first time),
adds Falling Wizard to your Applications, and running it again updates the game.
