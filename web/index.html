FALLING WIZARD
Features Spells Worlds Commands Install

A falling-sand wizard game where every pixel is alive. Burn forests, flood caves, summon tornadoes and black holes, then open a portal to a planet nobody has ever seen.

Click and drag to play
Terminal — install on macOS 
$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xandertaylorx/falling-wizard/main/web/install.sh | bash

Paste it into Terminal. It builds the game from source on your Mac (a couple of minutes the first time), adds Falling Wizard to your Applications, and gives you a falling-wizard command. Run it again any time to update.

A WORLD MADE OF PIXELS

Creatures everywhere. Lions pounce, demon eyes watch you, skeletons throw bones.
Black holes bend the light around them and swallow everything, rock included.
Oceans, beaches and volcanoes that erupt straight out of the sea.
Ray-traced lighting. Lava lights up caverns with real shadows.
Weather and nights. Storms, fog, snowfall, sandstorms and lightning that hunts trees.
Fly to space. Break the atmosphere into low gravity and floating alien islands.

EVERY GRAIN SIMULATED

Sand piles, water levels, oil floats and explodes in big pools, acid eats rock, lava turns water to steam and stone.

INFINITE WORLD

Forests, jungles, plains, deserts, tundra and oceans on top. Caves, ruins and lava caverns below. Sky isles above.

REACTIVE DEATHS

Fire turns foes to ash, lightning X-rays their skeleton, frost shatters them, acid melts them into goo.

COMMAND LINE

Press T to chat or / for commands with Minecraft-style suggestions and Tab completion.

SIXTEEN SPELLS

A GALAXY OF PLANETS

Find a portal structure, tune your Portal Wand to one of the thirty nearest planets, and shoot. Every planet is generated from its place in an endless galaxy, with its own ground, seas, plants, gravity, sky and creatures.

Choose a destination from the thirty nearest planets. Earth is always on the list.
Lightspeed travel through the galaxy to your new world.
Toxic worlds of giant glowing mushrooms and acid seas.

TWENTY-FOUR CREATURES

Tame one by casting Seeds of Life straight at it. Pets follow you and fight for you.
