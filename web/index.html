Falling Wizard

A wizard game for the Mac where everything is made of simulated grains. This box runs the same rules. Draw in it.

Try oil on water, then fire
curl -fsSL https://fw.crooli.com/install.sh | bash

Paste it into Terminal. It builds the game on your Mac, puts it in Applications, and running it again updates it.

What reacts with what

Nothing is scripted. Each grain follows a few rules, and they add up.

  • +
    Water puts out an ordinary fire and boils to steam doing it. The steam rises, cools and rains back down.
  • +
    Oil floats on water and burns on top of it. Throw water on a grease fire and it flashes to steam and spits the flames higher. Smother it with sand instead. A big enough pool of oil goes up in one bang.
  • +
    Lightning that hits water runs through the whole connected pool and shocks whatever is swimming in it. It also splits the water, and hydrogen bubbles rise to the surface and wait for a spark. Metal and gold conduct too.
  • +
    Lava meeting the sea throws off steam explosions. It cools into basalt, or glassy obsidian where water quenches it from above.
  • +
    Wood burns down to ash, which is light enough to float on water. Sand and dirt dropped on a flame put it out.
  • Fire needs air. It races through jungle worlds with thick, rich atmospheres, sputters on toxic ones, and dies at once on airless moons or above the atmosphere. Open water freezes on ice worlds and boils away on hot ones.
  • +
    Volcanoes rumble for a few seconds, blow out their plug, then fountain lava and lava bombs. Magma spills over the rim and runs down the slopes, ash rains out of the plume, and lightning flickers inside it. On ice worlds they erupt water and snow.

Spells

Nineteen of them, one per key. Lightning, Fireball and Frost do one thing when you tap and another when you hold.

Creative mode gives you unlimited mana, so the beams never run dry. There's also a sandbox brush (B) with every material in the game.

Other planets

Fly high enough and the sky turns to stars. Every planet has its own atmosphere height and colour, its own suns, and its own neighbours hanging in space. Find a portal structure, tune the Portal Wand with a right click, and warp to any of an endless galaxy of worlds, each with different ground, seas, gravity, air and creatures.

Get the game

It runs on macOS. Rust gets installed if you don't have it, and the first build takes a couple of minutes.

curl -fsSL https://fw.crooli.com/install.sh | bash

Then open Falling Wizard from Applications, or type falling-wizard in Terminal.
